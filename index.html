Research

My work spans real-time video restoration, few-step language generation, and causal world models. Below are selected publications and manuscripts, followed by ongoing research.

InstantViR: Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solver with Distilled Diffusion Prior
Weimin Bai*, Suzhe Xu*, Yiwei Ren, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
CVPR, 2026   Highlight (top 3%)
arXiv / Paper / Project / Presentation

We distill a video diffusion prior into a causal autoregressive student for one-step video restoration, without external paired clean/degraded training data or iterative test-time optimization. LeanVAE integration enables real-time streaming inpainting, deblurring, and super-resolution, with up to 100× speedup over iterative solvers. I co-led method development, implementation, and experiments, and presented the work at CVPR.

InstantViR++: Improved Posterior Distillation for Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solving
Suzhe Xu*, Weimin Bai*, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, 2026
* Equal contribution.

I study cross-VAE posterior distillation and the balance between measurement consistency and generative supervision. Bidirectional latent conversion queries the frozen teacher and returns predictions to student coordinates; a stop-gradient surrogate supplies the student-space update. Controlled comparisons investigate perceptual and temporal behavior, alongside cyclic measurement-consistency weighting.

Ongoing Research

Xiaohongshu (RED), Dots Sep. 2026–present

Native few-step language generation. I establish FLM cross-entropy/MSE baselines and adapt MeanFlow and simplified consistency training. Using 1/2/4-step generation, Gen PPL, MAUVE, repetition rates, and samples, I study the gap between training objectives and generation quality, and the trade-offs among quality, diversity, and computation.

Distribution-matching distillation. I investigate brightness degradation and structural loss in JiT-based image distillation. Controlled experiments with exact finite-support scores, direct pixel optimization, and generator updates distinguish score-estimation, optimization, and parameterization effects.

Peking University: Streaming World Models Ongoing

In an industry-collaborative research project, I develop four-step block-autoregressive world models for driving videos, with clean KV caching, self-forcing rollouts, and multi-view training.

Peking University: Multi-Agent World Model Post-Training and Evaluation Ongoing

I adapted Cosmos3-Edge for native dual-view, action-conditioned multi-agent generation, integrating independent action tokens and Gamma Simplex four-axis positional encoding to represent agent identity, time, and space. I completed staged training with a 93-to-189-frame curriculum on 16 A100 GPUs across two nodes.

I investigated temporal alignment and generation fidelity through comparisons with the official Gamma teacher, aligning initial conditions, frame rates, prediction horizons, and sampling steps. After 1,000 steps of 20 FPS adaptation, fixed-protocol validation loss decreased by 22.5% (2.143 → 1.662), while material and geometric errors persisted despite improved loss.
