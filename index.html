My work spans real-time video restoration, few-step language generation, and causal world models. Below are selected publications and manuscripts, followed by ongoing research.

InstantViR: Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solver with Distilled Diffusion Prior
Weimin Bai*, Suzhe Xu*, Yiwei Ren, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
CVPR, 2026   Highlight (top 3%)
* Equal contribution.
arXiv / Paper / Project / Presentation

We distill a video diffusion prior into a causal autoregressive student for one-step video restoration, without external paired clean/degraded training data or iterative test-time optimization. LeanVAE integration enables real-time streaming inpainting, deblurring, and super-resolution, with up to 100× speedup over iterative solvers. I co-led method development, implementation, and experiments, and presented the work at CVPR.

InstantViR++: Improved Posterior Distillation for Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solving
Suzhe Xu*, Weimin Bai*, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, 2026
* Equal contribution.

I study cross-VAE posterior distillation and the balance between measurement consistency and generative supervision. Bidirectional latent conversion queries the frozen teacher and returns predictions to student coordinates; a stop-gradient surrogate supplies the student-space update. Controlled comparisons investigate perceptual and temporal behavior, alongside cyclic measurement-consistency weighting.

Research in Progress

Prometheus: Causal Few-Step Streaming World Models for Autonomous Driving

Peking University · Industry-collaborative research · Jun. 2026–present
Working title. Manuscript in preparation; targeting submission to CVPR 2027.

I develop few-step causal world models for driving videos. I implemented a three-view pipeline combining chunk-causal fine-tuning, four-step causal consistency distillation, and Self-Forcing distribution matching distillation, with synchronized multi-view rollouts and clean-history KV caching. The implementation covers single-, three-, and five-view configurations; current five-view work extends causal distillation to 101-frame sequences.

Multi-Agent World Model Post-Training and Evaluation

Peking University · Ongoing
Research in progress; targeting submission to CVPR 2027.

I study multi-agent world models with independent per-agent action conditioning and native dual-view generation. I adapted Cosmos3-Edge and trained with a 93-to-189-frame curriculum. Through comparisons under matched frame rates, prediction horizons, and sampling budgets, I examine temporal consistency and discrepancies between training loss and visual fidelity. Planned work investigates cross-backbone distillation toward causal few-step generation.
