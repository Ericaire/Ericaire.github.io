InstantViR: Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solver with Distilled Diffusion Prior
Weimin Bai*, Suzhe Xu*, Yiwei Ren, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
CVPR, 2026   Highlight (top 3%)
* Equal contribution.
arXiv / Paper / Project / Presentation

We distill a video diffusion prior into a causal autoregressive student for one-step video inpainting, deblurring, and super-resolution, without external paired clean/degraded training data or iterative test-time optimization. With LeanVAE, InstantViR runs at over 35 FPS and achieves up to a 100× speedup over iterative solvers. I co-led method development, implementation, and experiments, and presented the work at CVPR.

InstantViR++: Improved Posterior Distillation for Real-Time Video Inverse Problem Solving
Suzhe Xu*, Weimin Bai*, Jinhua Hao, Ming Sun, Wenzheng Chen, He Sun
Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, 2026
* Equal contribution.

I study cross-VAE posterior distillation and the balance between measurement consistency and generative supervision. Bidirectional latent conversion queries the frozen teacher and returns predictions to student coordinates; a stop-gradient surrogate supplies the student-space update. Controlled comparisons investigate perceptual and temporal behavior, alongside cyclic measurement-consistency weighting.

DriveForge: An Open Foundation for Multi-Camera Driving Video Synthesis and Real-Time Simulation
Tianle Chen*, Yizao Tang*, Jun-Hao Wang*, Suzhe Xu*, Yi-Yang Tian*, Qiyu Dai*, Mengyu Chu, Peng-Shuai Wang, Baoquan Chen, Wenzheng Chen
Submitted to CVPR 2027
* Equal contribution.

We developed DriveForge for controllable multi-camera driving video generation. I built its causal distillation pipeline to turn a 7B bidirectional model into a four-step autoregressive generator. After chunk-causal fine-tuning and consistency distillation, I used Self-Forcing DMD to train the student to continue from its own predictions, including earlier mistakes. At four sampling steps per block, the final training stage reduced 16-frame FVD from 79.63 to 21.32. The system generates five synchronized camera streams at 10 Hz at 832 × 480 per view on four NVIDIA A800 GPUs.

Multi-Agent World Model Post-Training and Evaluation

Peking University · Ongoing
Research in progress.

I study multi-agent world models with independent per-agent action conditioning and native dual-view generation. I adapted Cosmos3-Edge and trained with a 93-to-189-frame curriculum. Through comparisons under matched frame rates, prediction horizons, and sampling budgets, I examine temporal consistency and discrepancies between training loss and visual fidelity. Planned work investigates cross-backbone distillation toward causal few-step generation.

Research Internships

DepthCore (AI startup) Oct. 2026–present

Research Intern · Mentor: Wenzheng Chen

I distilled a 1.84B-parameter stereo depth model into a 34.4M-parameter student by aligning features and cross-view relationships. On one RTX PRO 5000 GPU, its median forward pass took 4.71 ms per 320 × 720 stereo pair. Starting from the same student checkpoint, I compared five disparity refiners and profiled two of them to measure the latency cost of improving depth accuracy.

The training pipeline sampled from 23.1 million synthetic stereo pairs. Parallel decoding, prefetching, and shared caching improved measured eight-GPU distillation training throughput by approximately 4.5×. Our technical report on the stereo foundation model is in preparation.

Rednote (Xiaohongshu), Dots Studio Aug.–Oct. 2026

Multimodal Research Intern · Mentor: Weijian Luo

Native few-step language generation. I compared FLM baselines, MeanFlow, and simplified consistency training for 1/2/4-step generation. After transferring weights between two models, I found that their denoising predictions closely matched on the same input, yet their outputs diverged over a full sampling run. I tested how self-conditioning and the sampling schedule affected that gap.

Distribution-matching distillation. I investigated how score-difference processing and auxiliary score estimation affect one-step diffusion distillation, using controlled CIFAR-10 experiments to assess sample quality, diversity, and training stability.

Kuaishou, Multimodal Foundation Models Group Jan.–Jun. 2026
